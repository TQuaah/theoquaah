RESEARCH DIRECTION

How can organizations use technology strategically?

My research interests sit at the intersection of technology management, digital transformation, information systems and operations management.

I am particularly interested in how organizations in emerging economies can strategically adopt and manage digital technologies to improve decision-making, operational efficiency, organizational performance and stakeholder value.
